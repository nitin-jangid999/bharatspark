Privacy Policy for BharatSpark

Last updated: September 22, 2026

BharatSpark ("the App", "we", "our", "us") is a college companion mobile application built to help students of SKIT Jaipur and, in future, other Indian engineering colleges with notes, attendance tracking, community discussions, placement resources, and an AI-powered study assistant. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using BharatSpark, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use the App:

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and password (securely handled via Firebase Authentication) to create and manage your account.
  • Profile Information: Basic details you choose to provide, such as your name and college/branch information, used to personalize your experience within the App.
  • User-Generated Content: Content you create or submit inside the App, including community posts, questions, answers, comments, and notes you upload or interact with. This content is stored using Firebase Firestore.
  • Attendance Data: If you use the Smart Attendance feature, the attendance data you paste or enter (e.g., from your college ERP report) is processed and stored to calculate statistics and provide the "What If" simulator, so you can track your attendance over time.
  • AI Chat Interactions: Messages you send to the in-app AI Chat feature are sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate responses. We do not use these conversations for any purpose beyond providing you the AI chat functionality.
  • Notifications Data: To deliver announcements and personal notifications (such as replies to your community questions), we store minimal data required to route these notifications to your device.
  • Device and Usage Information: Standard technical information such as device type, operating system version, and app usage patterns, which may be automatically collected to help us maintain and improve the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate your identity
  • To provide core features such as notes access, attendance tracking, community discussions, timetable, CGPA calculation, and placement resources
  • To power the AI Chat feature using Google's Gemini AI
  • To send you relevant notifications (admin announcements and personal updates, such as replies to your posts)
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the App, including preventing unauthorized access
  • To improve, debug, and develop new features for the App

3. Third-Party Services

BharatSpark uses the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Used for authentication, database storage (Firestore), and app infrastructure. See Firebase's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini AI: Used to power the in-app AI Chat feature. Messages sent to this feature are processed by Google's AI services. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: BharatSpark may display advertisements through Google AdMob to support the free availability of the App. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show relevant ads. See Google AdMob's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase's cloud infrastructure (Google Cloud Platform), hosted in the asia-south1 (Mumbai) region. We implement Firestore security rules to restrict access — for example, only you can edit or delete your own community posts, and administrative features are restricted to authorized accounts only. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share data with the third-party services listed above (Firebase, Google Gemini AI, and Google AdMob if enabled), strictly to operate the App's features.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may update or correct your profile information within the App at any time.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.
  • You may choose not to use the AI Chat feature if you do not wish your queries processed by Gemini AI.

7. Children's Privacy

BharatSpark is intended for use by college students and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
